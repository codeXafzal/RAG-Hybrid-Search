EMPLOYEE EXPENSE AND REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Meal Reimbursement
Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim meal expenses up to ₹800 per day. Original bills or valid digital receipts must be submitted with the expense claim.

2. Travel Reimbursement
Economy-class train tickets and economy-class domestic flight tickets are eligible for reimbursement when approved in advance by the employee's manager. Employees must provide tickets and payment receipts as supporting documents.

3. Expense Submission Deadline
Employees must submit expense claims within 15 calendar days after completing their business trip. Claims submitted after this period require special approval from the Finance Manager.

4. Approval Process
All expense claims must be reviewed and approved by the employee's reporting manager before Finance processes the reimbursement. Finance normally processes approved claims within seven working days.

5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Personal shopping, entertainment subscriptions, traffic fines, and expenses without valid receipts are not reimbursable under this policy.

6. Mileage Reimbursement
Employees using their personal vehicles for approved business travel may claim ₹12 per kilometre for car travel and ₹5 per kilometre for motorcycle travel. The claim must include the travel date, destination, business purpose, and distance travelled.
